About

I'm a Computer Science student at Rutgers University – New Brunswick, expecting to graduate in May 2028. For nearly three years I've worked at Amazon's LGA5 facility in Edison, NJ, as a Sortation Associate and, more recently, a Process Guide in the DDU Dispatch operation — routing packages to the right trucks, enforcing lane cutoffs, and troubleshooting exceptions in real time on a high-volume floor.

That operations background is what makes my software projects different from the usual tutorial clones: I model processes I've actually run, not ones I read about. Alongside my CS coursework I've also been working through corporate finance — time value of money, bond valuation, DuPont analysis — which shows up in one of the projects below.

Projects

Warehouse Dispatch Scheduler

A Java simulation of a warehouse sortation and dispatch operation — the same process I run every shift. Routes inbound packages to dispatch lanes by destination zone, enforces truck weight capacity and cutoff times, prioritizes time-critical freight, and reports an end-of-shift on-time dispatch rate.
